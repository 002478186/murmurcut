Fix the words

Whisper mishears names and slang. Replace a word everywhere, or paste the script you read: murmurcut keeps the timing of your voice and uses your exact words, in any language or mix like Hinglish. Each script line becomes one caption line.

      B-roll

      murmurcut finds free photos for the main things you talk about, and picks the one that best fits what you said. When nothing fits, it shows the words as a text card instead. Change any search, swap the photo or upload your own.

      An AI reads each sentence and searches for what it shows, for example "skyline mumbai" instead of just "Mumbai". It runs in this tab and downloads a language model of about 500–800 MB the first time.

        Make an avatar video

        The avatar reads your script in their own voice, then murmurcut adds captions and b-roll as usual. Voices are English. The first time downloads the voice model (about 90 MB); recording takes a few seconds per sentence.

        Clean up

        Music

        No music.

          Search finds Creative Commons music (Jamendo, Freesound and others) that allows commercial use and changes. Music dips automatically while you talk, and its credit is added to the credits in B-roll.

          Dubbing

          Translates the captions and speaks each sentence with a free text-to-speech voice, timed to yours. English captions translate best. It's a generic voice, not yours, and the lips won't match. Downloads about 40–150 MB per language the first time.
